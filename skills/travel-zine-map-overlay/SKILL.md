Title: Travel Zine Map Overlay
Overview: Transform a travel photograph into a restrained, indie magazine (Editorial Photography / Travel Zine) geographic visual piece by compositing a transparent, authentic map layer over the original image's negative space without altering the underlying photograph.
Shooting Location: Priority 1 - Inspect original photo EXIF metadata for GPS coordinates; Priority 2 - Custom manual input if no GPS metadata is present.
Core Requirements:
1. Preserve the original photograph intact without recreating people, buildings, mountains, or sky, and without altering original colors, lighting, or shadow detail.
2. Add authentic geographic map elements matching the shooting location, such as coastlines, city streets, mountain terrain, elevation contour lines, or hiking trails, automatically selecting the map mode (urban, mountain, coastal) based on the photograph.
3. Render the map as a transparent PNG layer overlay rather than a traditional rectangular map card.
4. Place the map primarily within negative space (sky, sea surface, or uncluttered areas) without obstructing the main photographic subject.
5. Scale the map to occupy approximately 15%-25% of the frame, adjusting flexibly based on composition.
6. Use fine line weights, a low-saturation color palette, sparse English place names, and refined cartographic markers.
7. Mark the shooting location with a single small red dot; if exact GPS coordinates are unavailable, mark only the approximate general area and do not fabricate precise coordinates.
8. Preserve the original aspect ratio of the photograph without cropping into a forced square.
9. Keep the overall aesthetic restrained and natural with a subtle paper print texture, avoiding large titles or excessive decorative elements.
